How LifeLink Works

The 4-step emergency workflow engineered for rapid response and clinical accuracy.

STEP 1
📝

1. Register

Donors register their blood group, organ donation consent, current coordinates, and contact details in the secure database.

STEP 2
🚑

2. Request

Hospitals or patient caretakers submit an emergency request detailing blood group, required organ, location, and urgency.

STEP 3
⚡

3. Match

The system computes ABO/Rh compatibility, organ availability, and precise Haversine geographic distance (in km).

STEP 4
📢

4. Alert

Top compatible nearest donors are ranked and notified instantly via the emergency dispatch gateway with distance mapping.

📖 System Architecture & Engineering Specifications

Mathematical formulas, medical compatibility models, and algorithmic implementation details.

1. Geographic Distance Calculation (Haversine Formula)

The Haversine formula determines the great-circle distance between two points on a sphere given their longitudes and latitudes. In LifeLink, it calculates the physical distance in kilometers between the patient’s emergency location and registered donor locations.

Mathematical Formulation:
a = sin²(Δφ / 2) + cos(φ₁) ⋅ cos(φ₂) ⋅ sin²(Δλ / 2)
c = 2 ⋅ atan2( √a, √(1 − a) )
Distance (d) = R ⋅ c   [where R = 6,371 km (Earth's mean radius)]
• φ₁ and φ₂ are latitudes of patient and donor in radians.
• Δφ = (lat₂ - lat₁) and Δλ = (lon₂ - lon₁) in radians.

2. Clinical Red Blood Cell & Organ Compatibility Matrix

Transfusion safety requires strict matching of antigen-antibody interactions. The LifeLink matching algorithm enforces the medical standards:

Patient (Recipient) Blood Group Permissible Donor Blood Groups Clinical Classification
O-O-Can only receive O- (Universal RBC Donor)
O+O- O+Can receive O- and O+
A-O- A-Can receive O- and A-
A+O- O+ A- A+Can receive O and A types
B-O- B-Can receive O- and B-
B+O- O+ B- B+Can receive O and B types
AB-O- A- B- AB-Can receive all Rh-negative types
AB+All 8 Blood TypesUniversal Recipient (Can safely receive any blood type)

3. Multi-Criteria Ranking Algorithm

When an emergency request is processed, donors are evaluated through a 3-tier cascade:

Tier 1: Availability Check

Donors with available == "Available" are prioritized over unavailable candidates.

Tier 2: Exact Match Bonus

Exact blood group matches (e.g. O+ to O+) receive higher rank preference than cross-compatible groups (e.g. O- to O+).

Tier 3: Proximity (Distance km)

Candidates within identical compatibility tiers are sorted in ascending order of Haversine distance.
